# The Wildpoldsried dossier: legal since June, and nobody has crossed

_Published live (2026-11-09). One of seven mid-climb dossiers and the second abroad. The survey and ranking are in `NOTES-mid-climb-survey.md`. **Process honesty:** desk artefact, single-seat, no adversary, read partly through German-language sources. Tags: **[verified]** means fetched live on 2026-11-09 with the source named; **[desk]** means inference._

## The sharpest objection, on the face

**Germany's own community energy movement says the new law makes the bridge uneconomic.** Energy sharing under §42c EnWG took effect on 1 June 2026 **[verified — pv magazine, 1 Jun 2026]**. On the same day, an alliance including the Bündnis Bürgerenergie, Bürgerwerke, EWS Schönau and Naturstrom said that supplying members directly with local power is "complex and economically unattractive". They said local power carries most of the burdens of conventional supply, and they expect no broad market uptake in the short term **[verified — pv magazine, 1 Jun 2026]**. The legislative memorandum itself calls it "not a mass business" in the short to medium term **[verified — search of German press]**. So Wildpoldsried may be standing at the river with a bridge that charges too much to cross. That would be the bridge test's own failure mode: a *legal* bridge that is not *evidently better*.

## The village as found

Wildpoldsried, Oberallgäu, Bavaria: 2,586 residents (31 Dec 2025) **[verified — Wikipedia]**.

- **Generation far beyond demand.** It produced 321% more energy than it needed by 2011, and was later reported at five times its use. It has nine wind turbines (2021), four biogas digesters with a fifth being built, three small hydro plants, solar on 190 private households and on nine community buildings, and a district heat network with 42 connections **[verified — Wikipedia]**.
- **Still building.** On 2 July 2026 renergie Allgäu presented a biogas "Satellit" to the village, a CHP unit with a large gas store, pitched as "lokale Energie statt Import" (local energy instead of imports) and "Wertschöpfung bleibt in der Region" (the value stays in the region) **[verified — wildpoldsried.de, presentation 20260702]**. The shape is heat-led. Germany does not have the GB heat-network trap the ratchet cell names, so this does not transfer verbatim.
- **What is absent:** any energy-sharing community that the desk could find. Searches found no named village-level §42c project anywhere in Germany as of November 2026. One unverified forum claim mentions a service-provider model near Aschaffenburg **[verified absence in search, not a census]**.

## Distance to the river

**The law has reached the bank, but the economics have not.** Wildpoldsried has more surplus per head than any other place in this survey, an ownership culture that pays solar households dividends, and as of 1 June 2026 a legal route to share electrons over the public grid inside one balancing area. What it lacks is a reason that beats the feed-in tariff, and the Bündnis is lobbying for exactly that: grid-fee credit, a simpler electricity-tax exemption and pooling models **[verified — pv magazine]**. This is the survey's clearest case of a **political-contest wall** (the ratchet cell's walls ledger). It is not a frame-artefact. The ladder's own discipline applies: no rung prices off a policy that has not yet arrived.
