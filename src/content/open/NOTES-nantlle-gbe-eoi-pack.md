# The Nantlle GBE EoI pack — submission-ready, entity undecided

_Published live (2026-07-04). The owner said yes to submitting a Great British Energy Expression of Interest for Nantlle, per `NOTES-nantlle-dossier.md`. This document is the submission pack: the live process as fetched today, every field's content drafted from the dossier, and the one decision this desk cannot make on the owner's behalf. **Process honesty, same tagging as the dossier:** **[verified]** = fetched live 2026-07-04, source named; **[dossier]** = carried from `NOTES-nantlle-dossier.md`'s own tags without re-checking; **[desk]** = drafted here, unchecked in the world. This document does not submit anything. A government form asserting an organisational identity is an act only a human with standing to make that assertion can take — that is the entity question below, and it is left open on purpose._

## 1. The live process, checked today

**Form:** [gbe.gov.uk/express-your-interest](https://www.gbe.gov.uk/express-your-interest) (linked from [gbe.gov.uk/express-interest](https://www.gbe.gov.uk/express-interest)) **[verified — fetched 2026-07-04]**.

**Who may submit.** The form's own framing: *"Local and community energy organisations and developers."* No individual-applicant category is named, but the form does not gate on organisational status either — it asks for a person's name, an organisation name (which can be answered "N/A" or with an informal descriptor), and separately asks *"If you're enquiring on behalf of a specific place or community, what is the postcode?"* — a place-based enquiry is a recognised path distinct from an organisation's own submission **[verified]**. It is non-committing: *"Sending an EOI won't commit you to anything and it isn't a formal funding application — it's simply a first step."* **[verified]**.

**The date problem, printed rather than smoothed.** The form's own text states the window as *"Throughout the first half of 2026, we're running an Expression of Interest (EOI) process as part of our early market engagement"* **[verified — fetched 2026-07-04]**. Today is 4 July 2026 — into the second half. The form itself, fetched today, still renders live with no closed-notice and no visible end-date enforcement; nothing on the page states what happens when the window nominally ends, or whether it rolls over **[verified — absence, not a claim]**. This is the same class of failure the dossier's own opening objection warned about: a stale-window claim, caught or not caught depending on who checks and when. **Action, not assumption: re-open the form URL immediately before filing and confirm it still accepts submissions.** If it has been closed, the fallback is the Local Power Plan's Autumn 2026 GBE Capital Toolkit launch, and direct contact with GBE in the meantime **[dossier]**.

**Required fields — full list, fetched live** (grouped as the form groups them):

- *General information:* full name; organisation name; organisation type (dropdown: Local/Strategic Authority, Community benefit society, Co-operative, Social enterprise, Developer, Investor, Supplier, Other); role/job title; email; phone; mailing-list opt-in; organisation's location; postcode if enquiring on behalf of a specific place.
- *Enquiry interest:* commercially-sensitive-information flag; what you're interested in (Project advice/support, Partnership, Investment opportunities, Challenges, Other); free-text detail.
- *Project basics:* project/initiative name; technology types (Solar PV, Onshore Wind, Hydro, Storage, Flexibility/Demand-Side, Other); generation capacity (kW); storage size (kWh); flexibility capacity (kW); Commercial Operation Date year; expected project lifetime; current stage (Idea → Operational, eight-point dropdown).
- *Land status:* Heads of Terms / Option to Lease position (six-point dropdown).
- *Location:* postcode; local authority area.
- *Grid:* connection arrangement (behind-the-meter with/without export, export to network, not yet determined); connection voltage; connection status (No application → Completed); offer date/connection date if held.
- *Planning:* route (Permitted development, Local authority planning, DCO, Not sure, Other); stage (Not started → Consent granted/refused); free-text on location, status, timelines, risks.
- *Existing support:* yes/no; total funding received or committed; type of support.
- *Community ownership and local benefit:* how local people benefit (Community benefit fund, Community ownership, Energy bill reduction, Local energy supply, Energy advice, Investment in local area, Scholarships, Other); ownership share %; benefit to low-income/disadvantaged groups; free text.
- *Route to market:* Wholesale export, Offsite/Behind-the-meter PPA, Onsite self-consumption, Flexibility services, Storage arbitrage, Other.
- *Barriers and support:* main barriers (technical/legal/commercial expertise, capital access, offtake, planning, shared-ownership agreements, construction/operation, supply chain, Other); support required from GBE; estimated total CAPEX; CAPEX requirement from GBE; viability gap; funding gap to 5% return if non-commercial.
- *Timelines and attachments:* key timelines/decision dates; one PDF/Word attachment, 40MB max.
- *Anything else.*
- *Data sharing:* consent to share with the (England-only) Local Net Zero Hub — decline, since Nantlle is Welsh and the Hub network does not cover Wales **[dossier]**; GBE is a public body subject to FOIA/EIR, so mark anything commercially sensitive as such **[verified]**.

**No deadline within the window beyond the date problem above; no fee; single form per submission.**

## 2. The entity question — the owner's decision, not this desk's

The form wants one named person answering as one organisation. Three legitimate shapes exist; this pack drafts content usable under any of them, but only the owner can pick, because only the owner can assert (or decline to assert) standing on Ynni Lleu's or Yr Orsaf's behalf.

**A. The owner, personally, as an interested party.** Organisation name: "N/A" or a plain descriptor ("independent researcher / Nantlle valley"); organisation type: *Other*; enquiring on behalf of a specific place: yes, Penygroes/Talysarn postcode. Filed today, needs no one else's permission, costs nothing. Weakest fit to the form's project-detail fields, because the owner cannot assert Ynni Lleu's board-held facts (commissioning date, export arrangement, CAPEX) as their own organisation's answers — the honest move is to answer Part 3 at the level this desk actually knows (the dossier's [verified]/[desk] facts, tagged as such) and use the free-text fields to say plainly that this is third-party desk research surfacing a live project, and to flag that GBE should expect a submission from Ynni Lleu CBC directly.

**B. Drafted for Ynni Lleu CBC to submit under its own name.** Organisation type: *Community benefit society*. Strongest fit — Ynni Lleu is the actual holder of the actual project (311kW solar + 307kWh battery, £384k grant, commissioning in flight) and the only entity that can truthfully answer the CAPEX, commercial-operation-date, and grid-connection fields. Requires the owner to hand this pack to Ynni Lleu's board for review and adoption — which is the same conversation the dossier's off-desk item 1 already calls for (the three-questions ask). This is very possibly the same phone call doing both jobs at once.

**C. Drafted for Yr Orsaf (or the GwyrddNi/DEG regional programme) to submit as the coordinating civic body.** Organisation type: *Community benefit society* (Yr Orsaf is one) or *Other* (if filed as GwyrddNi/DEG). Best fit for the *development-grant* ask specifically — the roof census across Yr Orsaf's and Antur Nantlle's estates, the club-offer pricing work, Cwm Du's remaining pre-consent work — because that ask belongs to the valley's coordinating body, not to Ynni Lleu's generation project. Weaker fit for the generation-project fields (Yr Orsaf isn't the generator). Requires Siôn's (the GwyrddNi facilitator's) sign-off, the same contact named in the dossier's off-desk item 3.

**This desk's reading, not a decision:** the dossier's own ask already called for *both* Ynni Lleu and Yr Orsaf to file (dossier §"The ask", point 1) — so B and C are not really alternatives to each other, they are two separate filings with two separate owners-of-the-content. A is the only option that substitutes for B and C rather than complementing them, and it is the only one the owner can execute alone, today, without anyone else's authorisation. If the owner wants something filed *now*, in the current window, without waiting on a board conversation, A is the only route open to that constraint — with the honest caveat printed inside it.
