# The move you can see is not the move that matters

_Published live (2026-09-06). An internal objection: not a critique from another school, but a place where three of this work's own commitments pull against each other. Stated at full strength first, then judged: not fatal, not a tweak, a genuine open front with a physical floor inside it and a named repair route._

---

## The objection in one paragraph

The work says it does not need to measure option space globally, because it can evaluate moves locally: does this move open the reachable set or close it? That is the whole load-bearing answer to the measurability attack, and `option-space-measurability` says in terms that "the move-level measure is no longer open." But the work also says, in a node it grades as derived, that the destruction of option space is fast, local and legible, while its expansion is slow, systemic and conditional on substrate. Put those two side by side. The local read of a move sees the loss side now and the gain side never. It is not a noisy estimate of the true sign. It is biased toward the wrong sign, in exactly the direction the asymmetry names, on exactly the moves that matter. And the work has already shown this to itself: its own rig reproduced a move that reads as a gain locally and a collapse over the trajectory, and the only instrument that caught it was a full enumeration of the reachable cone, which is the global state measure the canon calls its one open problem. So the procedure the framework ships stands on the measure it says it does not have.

---

## The three commitments, in the work's own words

**One. Local is enough; global is the open problem.** `option-space-as-chess-moves`, graded derived: "Local move evaluation Δω(move) does not require global computation — it requires only directional comparison between the pre-move and post-move reachable sets." `option-space-measurability`, the framework's one open node: "The state-level measurement of option space remains a genuinely open research problem; the move-level measurement is already operational." The `/attack` page answers the vacuity attack on precisely this footing: "the move-level procedure is operational and decides consequential cases today."

**Two. Loss is legible now; gain is illegible until later.** `asymmetry-of-option-space-change`, graded derived: "Build-up processes are systemic and gradient-following; breakdown processes are local and gradient-releasing." Its formal definition puts τ_build over τ_break at two to six orders of magnitude for the configurations the objective protects. `configuration-generates-configuration` adds that expansion is "slow at any single step, compounding across steps, and conditional on the substrate not being removed faster than it accrues." `legibility-truth-tradeoff` says systems prefer what can be counted. `prevention-over-repair` says the most valuable moves "leave little trace."

**Three. The objective is a universal veto over an undefined term.** `viable-objective`: "reject any move that asymptotically empties R_living for some such class at some such horizon." Every major class of life, every relevant horizon. And in the same node's open questions: "The operational definition of 'major class of life'." `option-space-as-chess-moves` repeats it, and adds: "the criterion as currently stated is binary; reality is graded." `infinite-game` repeats it a third time: "an infinite game has no rule for what to do when continuation for one class of player requires ending it for another."

Each of these is defensible on its own. The objection is that they cannot all be true at once.

---

## Why they pull against each other

### The asymmetry makes the local read wrong-signed, not merely noisy

A move that consumes substrate to build substrate is the generative process the work calls value. Every road, enzyme, notation, standard, cell and rail in the canon's own examples is one. At the point of decision, the asymmetry says what such a move looks like: the consumption is local, fast and countable; the generation is systemic, slow and, by the work's own account, only visible once the substrate has compounded. A directional comparison of pre-move and post-move reachable sets made now, by the mover, from the mover's board, therefore reads the loss and not the gain. That is not a defect in the reader. It is what the asymmetry node says the world is like.

The chess metaphor hides this because chess has a fixed board. No move in chess creates squares. In the world the generative move creates squares, later, elsewhere, on boards the mover may not see, and the work knows this: polychess is its own extension of the metaphor. But polychess makes the problem worse, not better. Uncounted moves on boards you did not know you were playing are, by the asymmetry, mostly losses, and the uncounted gains arrive on boards that do not exist yet.

### The rig has already refused the derived node

`NOTES-ce-rig-v1-world-design.md`, section six, reproduces the counter-case on purpose: "a move that reads as an increase in possibility space measured locally and at an instant, and is a decrease measured globally over the trajectory." The miniature is eleven lines of arithmetic. Local, instantaneous count: Forage wins three to two. Trajectory cone: Rest wins three to two. The rig calls this the razor-blade and builds a detector for it: any move with Δ(local) > 0 and Δ(Ω trajectory-long) < 0.

Read what the rig had to do to catch it. Its measure is "global and trajectory-long by construction. There is no 'local Ω'; locality is retained only as a diagnostic." It enumerates the whole cone by breadth-first search, "no sampling, no estimate, no proxy," which is possible only because the world was built with about 150,000 state-round pairs. That is the global state measure. The canon's most serious experiment could not decide the case that matters with the instrument the canon says is sufficient, and could decide it only with the instrument the canon says is open. In a toy, the cone is enumerable. In a valley, it is not. The work has not yet said what it does in the valley.

### The flagship cases are only unambiguous at a grain the work has not fixed

The escape hatch is that "many consequential moves have unambiguous Δω signs even when global ω remains non-computable": mass extinction, monoculture, four degrees locked in. But the rule says any major class of life, and the grain of "class" is open in three nodes. Set it fine, at species or community, and every road and every ratchet cell forecloses some living configuration; the veto fires on the generative process itself and the framework becomes a rule against building. Set it coarse, at phylum, and no human move has ever emptied the reachable set of a phylum; four degrees passes, because Chordata persists. The wolf-and-deer example in `observer-relative-option-space` picks the coarse grain to make predation come out fine. The four-degrees example picks a finer one to make lock-in come out wrong. The same rule cannot do both unless the grain is a parameter, and the framework has not said what sets it. Three nodes each carry a third of this as a footnote. It is one question, and it is the question that decides how much the veto bites.

### The keystone hands the aiming job to the instrument that cannot see the gain

`pattern-intelligence-constraint` is graded forming and called load-bearing. It says intelligence is the search for the least-action path, and then concedes, inside the claim, that the cheap direction is foreclosure: breakdown is gradient-releasing, build-up needs continuous exergy input, so "by default, unaimed, or aimed at yield under a price that cannot see the sink, that is the cheaper direction." Its answer is aim: it points at `viable-objective` and `coordination-as-move-evaluation`. But the aiming rule is local move evaluation, and by the asymmetry that rule reads the loss side only. So the largest amplifier the work has ever named is to be aimed by an instrument the work's own physics says is miscalibrated in the amplifier's cheapest direction. `coordination-as-move-evaluation` then rules out the central planner on the grounds that it "inherits the non-computability of global ω," which is the very thing the rig needed.

### The register has felt this from three sides without naming the seam

`NOTES-recursive-fitness-and-bridge-test.md` calls the viable objective "defensive" and asks for an affirmative expansion-rate objective, then admits it "inherits that same measurement gap one level up." `the-closing-window` reports the rent-versus-protection line, which is the no-regret gate in use, as "still a judgment rather than a measurement." The rig design separates a score ledger that moves agents from a cone that judges trajectories and says they are "deliberately separate books." Five worked treatments have run the no-regret gate by hand, and none has logged a case where the local and trajectory reads disagreed and the trajectory read was acted on. The razor-blade has been reproduced only in the toy.

---

## What this objection is not

It is not the outside measurability attack. That attack says no global measure, therefore vacuous, and the `/attack` page answers it by leaning on the move-level procedure. This objection is that the lean has nothing under it. The move-level procedure was the thing that did not need the global measure, and the work's own asymmetry node and the work's own rig both say that on the cases that matter it does.

It is also not a claim that the no-regret criterion is wrong. On the loss side it is exactly right, and the asymmetry is the reason it is right. The objection is narrower and worse: the criterion is operational on one side of an asymmetry the framework itself says is two to six orders of magnitude wide, and the framework has presented one-sided operability as operability.

---

## Verdict

**Not fatal.** The value definition stands. The asymmetry stands. Observer-relative R_living stands. The substrate story stands. What falls is one sentence, "the move-level measure is no longer open," and one grade: `option-space-as-chess-moves` is over-graded at derived. That is a real cost, because the sentence is the work's answer to its best-known attack. It is not the framework.

**Not repairable by a tweak, and here is the floor.** You cannot make the slow-systemic gain legible at decision time. That illegibility is not a gap in the accounting; it is what the asymmetry is. Any rule that promises to read the gain of a generative move locally, now, from the mover's board, is promising to repeal the asymmetry, and the asymmetry is the better-established of the two commitments. So the repair cannot be a better local reader.

**A genuine open front, with a repair route the work already owns.** Three moves, none of which needs a new premise.

1. **Say it.** Regrade `option-space-as-chess-moves` to contested. Amend `option-space-measurability` to read: the move-level measure is operational on the loss side, and on the loss side only. Local Δω decides the veto. It does not decide the generative move. State this on the `/attack` page too, where the vacuity attack is answered, so the answer stops overclaiming.

2. **Fix the grain by the horizon.** The three footnotes asking what a major class of life is dissolve if the class is tied to the horizon the rule already carries. For a horizon T, a protected class is any living configuration whose τ_build exceeds T. That is the asymmetry node's own formal term, not a new one. It makes the veto bite exactly on what cannot be rebuilt inside the horizon and lets a hedgerow that regrows in a decade be crossed by a road judged on a decade. Predation passes because the deer population rebuilds within any horizon deer depend on. Four degrees fails because the sinks do not. The dial stops being a dial.

3. **Judge trajectories, not moves, with a reversibility budget.** The rig already does this and calls the two books separate. Make that the canon's procedure and name it honestly: not local Δω but a bounded-horizon cone with a loss-side veto. A legible local loss is admissible only where it is reversible within the τ of the gain it is spent to buy. The razor-blade detector becomes the discipline every treatment runs by hand: write the local read, write the trajectory read, and where they disagree the trajectory read wins and the disagreement is logged. That gives the treatments the one thing none of them has yet produced, a recorded razor-blade in a real domain.

What stays open after those three moves is the honest form of the problem: how wide a cone can be enumerated, or bounded, in a real domain, at a cost the domain can pay. That is a research front, it is the same front the rig is on, and it should be stated as such rather than left under a sentence that says the move-level measure is closed.

**Climb condition.** This objection is answered, not merely engaged, when one worked treatment logs a move where the local and trajectory reads disagreed and the design acted on the trajectory read. Until then the razor-blade lives only in the toy, and the toy is the strongest evidence the objection has.
