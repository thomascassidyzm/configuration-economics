## The sphere, and what the treatment actually is

Next in backlog order: **the other slate valleys** — Blaenau Ffestiniog, Nantlle, Llanberis, Corris — flagged as pattern-replication targets. Which forces a question the spec does not answer (spec-friction item 1): what is the *unit* here? Four valleys are not a design. The design under treatment is **replication itself**: the ratchet cell's rung 8 — cell-in-a-box, the kit + framework + shared back-office sold to the next community — pulled out of its parent ladder and treated as a first-class template with its own rungs, its own gate, and one valley as the worked instance. The fourth treatment is *the second cell*.

That makes this run the sharpest available discharge test for `the-cell-compounds`, and the prediction is registered **before** the fleet launches, so the evidence can falsify it: a new valley is **off-substrate by construction** — no shared billing rail, no accumulated trust, no wrapper to amend. The candidate therefore predicts that founding costs *recur* in each new valley (the quasi-founding reappears: a share offer, a local champion, a constitution written not amended). The observer-architecture note predicts the split precisely: **specification travels, continuity doesn't** — the artefact kit, the legal templates, the metering runbooks should transfer at near-zero marginal cost, while the trust, the membership roll, and the institutional wrapper should have to be re-grown valley by valley at close to full price. If the evidence shows second and later cells forming dramatically cheaper *across the board* — continuity included — the candidate's off-substrate prediction fails and it should say so on its face. If the evidence shows the split — kit cheap, trust expensive — both `the-cell-compounds` and the observer-architecture frame gain their first measured case.
