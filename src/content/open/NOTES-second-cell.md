## The sphere, and what the treatment actually is

Next in backlog order: **the other slate valleys** — Blaenau Ffestiniog, Nantlle, Llanberis, Corris — flagged as pattern-replication targets. Which forces a question the spec does not answer (spec-friction item 1): what is the *unit* here? Four valleys are not a design. The design under treatment is **replication itself**: the ratchet cell's rung 8 — cell-in-a-box, the kit + framework + shared back-office sold to the next community — pulled out of its parent ladder and treated as a first-class template with its own rungs, its own gate, and one valley as the worked instance. The fourth treatment is *the second cell*.

That makes this run the sharpest available discharge test for `the-cell-compounds`, and the prediction is registered **before** the fleet launches, so the evidence can falsify it: a new valley is **off-substrate by construction** — no shared billing rail, no accumulated trust, no wrapper to amend. The candidate therefore predicts that founding costs *recur* in each new valley (the quasi-founding reappears: a share offer, a local champion, a constitution written not amended). The observer-architecture note predicts the split precisely: **specification travels, continuity doesn't** — the artefact kit, the legal templates, the metering runbooks should transfer at near-zero marginal cost, while the trust, the membership roll, and the institutional wrapper should have to be re-grown valley by valley at close to full price. If the evidence shows second and later cells forming dramatically cheaper *across the board* — continuity included — the candidate's off-substrate prediction fails and it should say so on its face. If the evidence shows the split — kit cheap, trust expensive — both `the-cell-compounds` and the observer-architecture frame gain their first measured case.

### The replication record — cell-in-a-box has never been observed; something else has

- **Energy Local lists 36 clubs** (fetched live from the clubs page). **Correction to the second-commodity note**, which carried "~100 Energy Local-style clubs" as the addressable substrate: the listed count is 36; log at next register reconciliation. One club behaviour worth its own line: Corwen filled, **closed to new members, and now redirects to Glyndwr** — an adjacent club formed to absorb the overflow. A queue producing a second cell next door is the replication mechanism caught in the act.
- **The kit, as a self-serve artefact, does not exist.** Energy Local's own path for a new community is a Google form and two tiers of intro calls — no published requirements, costs, or timeline. The specification is real but it is not *shipped*; it is delivered through a relationship.
- **What actually replicates cells is a regional paid organ.** Cyd Ynni — a consortium formed 2017, run by DEG (Datblygiadau Egni Gwledig), members including Ynni Ogwen, Ynni Padarn Peris, Ynni Anafon, Egni Mynydd and Moelyci (DEG's own list; a secondary town-list from Community Energy England is garbled and flagged unreliable) — shares exactly the founding services: suitability assessment, usage analysis, contractor liaison, funding identification, grant-writing, and establishing Energy Local clubs. Funded £247k (National Lottery, 2017) and £188k (Welsh Government Energy Service, Feb 2023). And the tail fact that matters most: member groups have committed **£70k of their own surpluses** to local initiatives, including **£10k to start a brand-new community energy group in Llanfairfechan** — existing cells funding the next cell's rung 0 out of profits. Replication is observed; kit-sale is not. (DEG's own Cyd Ynni project page is password-protected — characterised from secondary sources, flagged.)

### The four valleys — four different rungs of the same ladder

- **Llanberis: asset, no club.** Ynni Padarn Peris: 55kW hydro on the Afon Goch, £280k community share raise in 2016 — **97% from local people, 90% of shareholders within 15 miles** — paying a steady 3% since 2017, community benefit fund projected ~£300k over 20 years. A Cyd Ynni member. No Energy Local club serves Llanberis: the valley has paid the *asset* founding cost and never taken the cheapest remaining step. Meanwhile the valley's largest void asset is spoken for and stalled: **Glyn Rhonwy**, a consented 99.8MW pumped-storage scheme in two disused slate pits (consented 2017 at ~£160m), has had its consent extended after "significant and unprecedented" capital-cost rises. Enclosed-and-stalled, with Dinorwig's 1.7GW humming inside the mountain next door.
- **Blaenau Ffestiniog: partnership, applications in flight.** Ynni Cymunedol Twrog with Y Dref Werdd and Cwmni Bro Ffestiniog: a £250k application for 136kW of solar plus batteries across 13 buildings (social enterprises, small businesses, charities) in Blaenau, Penrhyndeudraeth and Gellilydan, with a declared ambition to grow into a Community Energy Service Company — hydro plus district heat, in partnership with Energy Local and Gwynedd Council. The heat leg is already studied: the Tanygrisiau feasibility (Prospus, Oct 2023, WGES-funded, fetched and read in full) proposes a medium-temperature network for 44 social-landlord properties **sourcing heat from the outflow of the pumped-storage reservoir** — £1.55–2.3M phase one, against £2.92–6.1M for the borehole alternative. Two details for later: this is Blaenau's *third* commissioned energy study (a 2021 district-energy feasibility and a Sept 2023 ground/water-source study precede it), and the consultant's report-details page still reads "Skelmersdale" — templates travel, and travel carelessly.
- **Nantlle: civic rail, no energy.** Yr Orsaf / Siop Griffiths in Penygroes — a Community Benefit Society running a hub (café, offices, accommodation, repair café/make space) and community transport with three EVs; GwyrddNi climate assemblies (six social enterprises across five Gwynedd areas) convene here. A clean-energy mapping study for the valley "may soon be underway" — i.e. the valley stands at rung 0 of energy while already holding the *civic* substrate a founding usually has to grow.
- **Corris: the honest empty.** Population ~600; no community-energy vehicle discoverable; the nearest club activity is the Machynlleth/Dyfi cluster five miles south. Nothing found is a finding: this is the control valley, where the ladder has no local adopter unit yet.

### The regime — the election-contingent flag resolves, favourably and precariously

- **Senedd election, 7 May 2026:** Plaid Cymru 43 of 96 seats, minority government, Rhun ap Iorwerth First Minister; Reform UK 34; Labour 9; Greens enter with 2. Plaid's stated plan: **merge Trydan Gwyrdd Cymru, Ynni Cymru and the Welsh Government Energy Service into a single national energy company.** The ratchet cell's "election-contingent" flag on Ynni Cymru resolves: the politics landed on community energy's side, and the counterparty is about to be rebuilt — every programme name in this note may be a different organisation's letterhead within a year.
- **The money:** GB Energy committed £21.5m for community and public-sector projects in Scotland, Wales and NI (Local Power Plan, Feb 2026); ~£9.5m already into Wales; the £10m Ynni Cymru capital scheme is live; **Ymestyn grants must complete by 30 November 2026** — a genuinely short fuse. And the big one: up to **£1bn of direct GB Energy funding launching later in 2026, explicitly targeted at "underserved areas or where there has historically been low take-up"** — a targeting clause that deliberately routes money *away* from the compounding cells and toward the empty map. Corris-shaped places are about to become fundable in a way Bethesda-shaped places are not.
- **The wire:** SP Manweb's network is constrained (thermal, voltage, fault-level); new generation connects via flexible/ANM arrangements — connect now, accept curtailment when the network is full. The queue is managed, not gone.

### The slate voids — the research good is being bought retail, N times

No dedicated characterisation of the flooded slate-void system as a water or heat resource is discoverable — BGS holds general Wales hydrogeology references; coal has the Coal Authority's mine-water heat programme; slate has no equivalent. Green's premise survives at the strength "no discoverable dedicated study." But the sharper live finding is what exists *instead*: Blaenau alone has commissioned three overlapping feasibility studies in three years, each privately scoped, consultant-delivered, and non-cumulative. The characterisation is being purchased as retail fragments, valley by valley, instead of once as a regional commons.

### The waitlist scan — two queues, at two tiers

Run per the spec, function identified at the level the queuers experience it. **Tier one, households:** the Bethesda club's closed waitlist (verified in the ratchet-cell run) and Corwen's closed-redirect are queues for *cheaper electricity in the match windows* — bill relief, already banked as the domain's adoption experiment. **Tier two, communities:** Energy Local runs a standing register-interest mechanism for places without clubs (depth unpublished), and a new group formed the moment £10k and DEG support were offered (Llanfairfechan). The second queue is not for electricity; it is for the **founding service** — the grant-writing, feasibility and club-establishment labour that DEG sells. The spec's scan assumes one queue per domain; this domain queues at both tiers of the adopter unit, and the design below enters through both doors. (Spec-friction item logged.)
