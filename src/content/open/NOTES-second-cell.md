## The sphere, and what the treatment actually is

Next in backlog order: **the other slate valleys** — Blaenau Ffestiniog, Nantlle, Llanberis, Corris — flagged as pattern-replication targets. Which forces a question the spec does not answer (spec-friction item 1): what is the *unit* here? Four valleys are not a design. The design under treatment is **replication itself**: the ratchet cell's rung 8 — cell-in-a-box, the kit + framework + shared back-office sold to the next community — pulled out of its parent ladder and treated as a first-class template with its own rungs, its own gate, and one valley as the worked instance. The fourth treatment is *the second cell*.

That makes this run the sharpest available discharge test for `the-cell-compounds`, and the prediction is registered **before** the fleet launches, so the evidence can falsify it: a new valley is **off-substrate by construction** — no shared billing rail, no accumulated trust, no wrapper to amend. The candidate therefore predicts that founding costs *recur* in each new valley (the quasi-founding reappears: a share offer, a local champion, a constitution written not amended). The observer-architecture note predicts the split precisely: **specification travels, continuity doesn't** — the artefact kit, the legal templates, the metering runbooks should transfer at near-zero marginal cost, while the trust, the membership roll, and the institutional wrapper should have to be re-grown valley by valley at close to full price. If the evidence shows second and later cells forming dramatically cheaper *across the board* — continuity included — the candidate's off-substrate prediction fails and it should say so on its face. If the evidence shows the split — kit cheap, trust expensive — both `the-cell-compounds` and the observer-architecture frame gain their first measured case.

## The re-level — replication is not a shipping problem

The expansion pass's first question, per the spec: is this the right altitude? It wasn't. The backlog's framing — and the parent design's rung 8 — treats replication as a *shipping* problem: package the kit, sell it to the next valley, collapse setup from years to weeks. The evidence says the region never did that, and the observer-architecture frame says why: **specification delegates, continuity doesn't.** The templates, checklists and runbooks transfer at near-zero cost — and do not suffice, because what a new valley lacks is not documents but trust, a paid pair of hands, and a grant pipeline. The region's actual answer, running since 2017, is to **mutualise the continuity at a tier above the cell**: DEG/Cyd Ynni as a standing regional organ that carries the founding labour from valley to valley, funded once, amortised across every cell it serves. The trade-off the naive rung 8 couldn't dissolve ("kits are cheap but don't work; consultancy works but doesn't scale") dissolves at the higher altitude: the kit travels *inside* a shared organism. The cross-domain rhyme is old and load-bearing: this is how credit-union and co-operative federations replicated (a central body carrying founding services to each new society), and its absence is why forest school's unforced precipitation stayed thin. So the design under treatment re-levels: not *cell-in-a-box* but **the second-cell ladder** — the completion order for a valley, with the regional tier as rung 0 already standing.

## The ladder — the second cell

Eight rungs. The adopter unit shifts as the ladder climbs — a household, then a generator's board, then a valley's civic vehicle — each named at its rung, per the anti-gerrymander discipline. Every rung individually rational on day-one terms except where confessed; no rung requires the next; the institution does not arrive late this time because *it already arrived* — the consortium precipitated in 2017, and this ladder's constitutional event count is zero.

**Rung 0 — the regional organ, already paid.** DEG/Cyd Ynni standing; its per-valley output is the **valley dossier** — roof census, generator candidates, EPC clusters, grid headroom, the local figures that turn the parent template's placeholders into prices. Grant-funded (Ynni Cymru £10m; Ymestyn to Nov 2026), which rung 0 is allowed to be; no rung above cites a grant except where confessed below.

**Rung 1 — the household rungs, inherited verbatim.** The ratchet cell's rungs 1–3 (hermit-and-cylinder, party-wall dyad, free-roof anchor) transfer to any valley unchanged — pure specification, re-priced not re-designed. This is the measured half of the inheritance claim: what compounds across cells *without* shared continuity is exactly the paper.

**Rung 2 — the club around the standing asset (Llanberis's move).** Where a community generator already exists, the cheapest unclimbed rung in the region: an Energy Local club needs the generator's board to say yes (one decision-maker, better than export price from the first matched kWh), members who join on Bethesda's observed record (~24% average savings, clubs that fill and waitlist), and coordination labour bought from the regional organ, not volunteered. No share offer, no new capital, no founding act. Ynni Padarn Peris has been generating for nine years without this rung — a £0-capex completion sitting untaken, which is itself evidence the kit doesn't self-execute.

**Rung 3 — join the consortium, don't found the federation.** Cyd Ynni membership: shared grant-writing, procurement, back-office. Day-one rational (cheaper services immediately); the federation is consumed as a service, never founded as a cause.

**Rung 4 — the civic-rail entry (Nantlle's move) — the confessed rung.** Where a valley holds a civic CBS but no generation: dossier first, then the free-roof-anchor pattern across the civic estate (Ynni Twrog's 13-building shape). **Confession, printed per the grammar:** post-FiT, new community generation does not reliably clear on self-consumption alone; this rung leans on the live capital-grant windows, and its day-one rationality is bought by a subsidy regime with dates on it. In the FiT era this rung stood alone; today it doesn't, and a ladder that hid that would be marketing.

**Rung 5 — surplus seeding (the Llanfairfechan move, observed live).** Established cells fund the next valley's dossier from their own surpluses — £10k scale, already happening. **Second confession:** the self-interest here is thin (each new cell spreads the regional organ's fixed costs across more members — real, but nobody writes a cheque for that reason). This rung runs on the same civic-blended motive the energy run found in community-share capital. The design carries one civic rung and says so, rather than inventing a rationale.

**Rung 6 — the second commodity, on schedule.** Each valley that completes rungs 2–3 becomes the substrate the second-commodity treatment priced: mobility (Yr Orsaf's three EVs are a pre-adaptation), then others, at marginal founding cost. The parent treatments' verdicts flow through unchanged.

**Rung 7 — the research commons.** The slate-void characterisation bought **once**, regionally, instead of retail per valley: Blaenau's three overlapping studies in three years are the price signal, and valley N+1's dossier is cheaper the moment the shared study exists. Individually rational for the next commissioning valley (a share of a commons costs less than a fourth consultant), and the first standing artefact the regional tier owns beyond its back-office.

**Rung 8 — the voids (the headline, pre-demoted).** Quarry-water heat at Tanygrisiau scale, void storage at Glyn Rhonwy scale, "the slate valleys as one organism" — quotable, and not load-bearing. The ladder pays in full if no void is ever tapped. Filed as a free option per the grammar; every run's headline has been its weakest claim and this run declines to test the pattern.

## The clocks — typed before doctrine, per the spec

Three clocks, two of known type, one new:

- **Ymestyn, 30 November 2026** — a *closing window*, unusually short: capital grants that must be completed and commissioned inside seventeen months of this note. Doctrine per the energy run: move while it's open, base no economics on its renewal.
- **The £1bn direct-funding launch, "later in 2026"** — an *opening window* on a semi-known date, the curriculum run's type: money aimed explicitly at underserved, low-take-up areas. Doctrine: **have the dossier ready the day it opens** — Nantlle and Corris qualify as underserved; the regional organ should be holding finished dossiers for both when the scheme's front door opens.
- **The merger** — a type the spec's typology does not contain (spec-friction, and a candidate move): the **counterparty rebuild**. Trydan Gwyrdd Cymru, Ynni Cymru and the Welsh Government Energy Service are to become one national energy company; the clock is neither closing nor opening — the rail the design leans on is being *re-laid mid-run*. Doctrine, proposed: prefer signed trades to programme relationships; convert every live grant window into a contract before the machinery-of-government churn; treat programme names as scaffolding, not ground.

## The gate — one seat, three lenses, and the verdict that survives

**Degradation printed first, again:** the spec's load-bearing property — adversaries independent of the synthesiser — is not satisfied. One seat ran three adversarial lenses in sequence; what follows is honest attack but not independent attack, and the verdict is capped accordingly. The independent re-run is this treatment's condition zero.

**Lens one — the catchment.** The whole ladder is conditional on rung 0 existing, and rung 0 is not a document — it is a standing, funded regional organ that took a subsidy era and a decade to accrete. Inside the catchment (a region with a DEG-shape and civic-CBS density: Gwynedd, and whatever counts as its peers — *count unknown, a condition*), the ladder passes. Outside it, "replicate the pattern" collapses back into founding a regional organ — a founding act the bridge test forbids — and the backlog's pattern-export line (South Wales Valleys, Cumbria, Pennines) walks straight into this wall: **regions compound cells, and nothing yet observed compounds regions.** The gate passes the ladder inside its region and fails the export headline the backlog was hoping for; that sentence is the discrimination working.

**Lens two — the rationality audit.** Two of eight rungs are confessed (rung 4 grant-priced, rung 5 civic-blended). The grammar's clause 1 admits no exceptions, and the spec gives no confession budget (friction item), so the lens rules narrowly: the ladder is a true no-faith ladder **only for asset-holding valleys** — Llanberis-class completions, where every live rung clears on day-one terms with zero new capital. For empty valleys the ladder is honest but time-boxed: its rationality is bought by grant windows with published expiry dates, and the verdict must say "no-faith for completions, grant-priced for entries" rather than pretend one grade covers both.

**Lens three — the evidence audit.** The re-level's central claim (the regional organ is the replication mechanism) rests on one region's history, partly from secondary sources, with the primary page password-protected. And the honest timeline cuts against the strong reading: the first-generation cells (Ogwen, Padarn Peris, Anafon) were **FiT-era foundings that predate the consortium** — Cyd Ynni precipitated *from* the cell wave in 2017, and only then began seeding. The organ-as-mechanism claim therefore has exactly **one post-window mechanism observation (Llanfairfechan, £10k, outcome unknown)**. The structure survives; the evidence for it is n=1 with the result not yet in.

**Verdict: passes-with-conditions — one-seat.** The narrowed claim, stated exactly: *bridge-clean, capital-free, and immediately actionable for valleys already holding a community generation asset (the club-completion move); grant-priced and window-dated for civic-rail valleys entering cold; conditional throughout on a standing regional organ whose replication power has one unresolved observation; and false as a portable export to organ-less regions, where the founding problem this design routes around reappears at full strength.* Conditions: (0) independent adversary re-run; (1) ask Ynni Padarn Peris the nine-year question below before any claim about rung 2 ships; (2) track Llanfairfechan to an outcome — it is the design's only live test of rung 5; (3) count the catchment — how many UK regions actually hold a DEG-shape?; (4) have Nantlle and Corris dossiers ready before the £1bn window opens, or print that the doctrine wasn't followed.

## The sharpest objection the run could not answer — the nine-year untaken rung

Llanberis has held the asset, the precedent one valley north, consortium membership, and access to the kit **for nine years** — and the club rung sits untaken. The design reads this as "the cheapest unclimbed rung in the region." The objection reads it as evidence against the design's core: if a rung is individually rational, capital-free, and served by a paid regional organ, and it stays untaken for a decade *in the region that invented the pattern*, then rationality-plus-kit-plus-organ is demonstrably not sufficient — something unnamed is binding. One candidate answer is disquieting for the whole series: **the FiT contract.** A generator already collecting FiT payments on every unit may gain little from a club's matching arrangement; what looks from the ladder like an unclimbed rung may be, from the generator's side, a rung not worth climbing until the FiT expires. If that is the explanation, the region's map of "incomplete cells" partly redraws as "contented cells," the addressable substrate shrinks again, and the completion move quietly acquires a clock of its own (FiT expiries, 2035–2040). The run cannot resolve this from a desk; it is condition 1, and it attacks the design's *strongest* rung, which is exactly where the sharpest objection should live.

## The spec-friction log — where the spec was wrong, ambiguous, or missing a tool

This run's second deliverable: the first field test of `NOTES-treatment-spec.md`, operated *from the document*, logging every place it failed its operator. The spec's own climb condition asked for exactly this.

1. **The sphere→design gap (missing tool).** The spec defines the treatment unit but gives no rule for deriving a design from a backlog sphere entry. "Other slate valleys" is four places, not a template; a pre-step had to be invented (name the template the sphere implies) before stage one could run.
2. **Recursion silence (missing rule).** When the design under treatment is a rung of a prior treatment (this run gates the ratchet cell's rung 8), the spec says nothing about whether the parent's conditions flow down. This run treated parent verdicts as flowing through rung 6 untouched; a different operator could defensibly re-litigate them. The spec should choose.
3. **No grade for a one-seat gate (ambiguity, load-bearing).** The spec grades fleet degradation (print first, weaken verdict) but not adversary degradation. Its three verdict grades are precedents, not a closed set, and it never says whether the set is open. "Passes-with-conditions — one-seat" had to be coined; the spec should either admit new grades explicitly or close the list.
4. **The scan's single-queue assumption (wrong, mildly).** "Scan the domain for queues" assumes one queue per domain; this domain queues at two tiers of the adopter unit with two different functions. Upgrade: scan per tier.
5. **The clock typology's open/closed status (ambiguity).** A fourth clock type was needed. The spec presents three types as an inventory without saying whether new types are spec amendments or candidate moves. Treated here as a candidate move on `the-closing-window`; the spec should say which.
6. **Time-indexed walls (ambiguity).** Grid headroom is a physical floor this afternoon and a political contest over a DNO investment cycle. The three-class ledger has no rule for walls that change class with horizon; classified as floor-with-overlay and logged.
7. **The shifting adopter unit (gap in the gate).** The median test assumes a fixed adopter unit per design. A completion-order ladder legitimately changes unit per rung; the anti-gerrymander guard needed the invented extension (name the unit at each rung, median-test at that unit) to stay operable.
8. **"The institution arrives late" has no branch for "already arrived" (minor).** On a pre-precipitated substrate the clause is satisfied vacuously; the spec should say that consuming a standing institution counts as compliance, not as skipping the clause.
9. **No confession budget (gap, exploitable).** Clause 1 admits no exceptions; clause 4 licenses confessing what can't be paid for. Nothing says how many confessed rungs a ladder may carry before it stops being a no-faith ladder. This run carried two of eight and split the verdict by valley class — a rule the spec should own rather than each run improvising.
