# The Fintry dossier — owning output, not electrons

_Published live (2026-11-09). One of seven mid-climb dossiers; survey and ranking in `NOTES-mid-climb-survey.md`. **Process honesty:** desk artefact, single-seat, no adversary. Tags: **[verified]** fetched live 2026-11-09, source named; **[desk]** inference._

## The sharpest objection, on the face

**Fintry owns a share of a wind farm's *money*, not of its *electrons* — and a match club runs on electrons.** The trust holds "one fifteenth of the Earlsburn Wind Farm output" **[verified — localenergy.scot]**, a contractual stake inside a 37.5 MW commercial farm now operated by Nadara. The physical power almost certainly leaves under the farm's own offtake contract **[desk]**. So Fintry is the most *successful* case in this survey by income and possibly the *furthest* from the river by plumbing: a club would need Nadara's consent to carve the trust's fifteenth out of the farm's PPA, and nothing in the record says the agreement allows it.

## The village as found

Fintry, Stirling — about 700 people **[desk — not re-verified]**, the famous early Scottish case of a community buying into a commercial wind farm.

- **The stake.** One fifteenth of Earlsburn's output (commissioned 2007, 37.5 MW); most revenue went to loan repayment until 2023, when the loan cleared and all income came to the trust **[verified — localenergy.scot]**.
- **The income.** £430,000 transferred to the trust for 1 Oct 2024 – 31 Mar 2025; board minutes put turbine income "around £400,000" with plant maintenance ~£119k **[verified — FDT AGM report Feb 2026; minutes 6 Aug 2025]**.
- **The clock.** April 2026 minutes: wind farm income "expected to decline as ROCs … end in October"; the board is writing to Nadara, via lawyers, "to clarify position on decommissioning/repowering" and to "negotiate for a stake in any future developments" **[verified — FDT minutes 29 Apr 2026]**.
- **What the money does.** £1,000 household grants for heating, insulation and glazing since 2023; an £80k energy-efficiency pot and a £25k renewables-assistance grant aimed at oil-to-heat-pump switches; ~20% of households already on renewables; £100k of fuel payments over four years **[verified — localenergy.scot; minutes 29 Apr 2026]**.
- **What is absent:** a club. The nearest Energy Local club is **East Loch Lomond** (Balmaha, Drymen, Gartocharn), over the Campsies — not Fintry **[verified — energylocal.org.uk/clubs]**.

## Distance to the river

**Two locks, one of them owned by someone else.** Lock 1: the electrons (Nadara's PPA and the stake agreement). Lock 2: no local generator of the trust's own. But the clock is real and rare: **the ROC end in October 2026 is the moment export value per MWh falls and local-match value does not** — exactly ratchet-cell condition 3 (price off what persists, never off subsidy) showing up as a dated event. And the repowering negotiation is open *now*: a stake clause in a repowered Earlsburn is the cheapest moment ever to write "a share of output deliverable to a local match pool" into a contract. That is a sentence in a lawyer's letter the board is already sending, not a founding act.
